About the club

Sundar Sanskruti Sports Club brings residents together through cricket, creating a positive sporting culture in our society.

What we do

We plan and host our annual society cricket tournament and related events — from registrations to match-day operations, awards and celebrations.

  • Season planning, fixtures & scheduling
  • Team management and communications
  • Match scoring and simple stats tracking
  • Event galleries and highlights
